TapTone helps colorblind users distinguish colors better. This privacy policy explains what information the TapTone app uses and how it is handled.

**In short:** TapTone works entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send your photos, camera images or test answers anywhere.
{: .callout}

## Permissions TapTone Asks For

- **Camera** — used by the Detect tab to name the color in the center of the camera view, by the Simulate tab to show the camera feed through color vision simulations, and by the Assist tab when you choose to take a photo.
- **Photo library** — used only when you choose a photo to apply assistive color filters to. TapTone can only see the photo you pick.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in your device's Settings. The rest of the app keeps working without them.

## Photos And Camera Images

Photos and live camera images are processed on your device, in memory. TapTone does not upload, save, sell or share your photos or camera images, and does not keep them after you leave the screen.

## Color Vision Test

The Adult, Kids and Shapes color vision tests run on your device. Your answers and score are used only to show your result on screen. They are not stored after you leave the test and are never sent off your device.

## Clipboard

When you tap the copy button in the Detect tab, TapTone copies the detected color's hex code (for example `#D65C40`) to your clipboard. TapTone does not read your clipboard.

## Preferences Stored On Your Device

TapTone saves a few settings on your device so the app remembers them:

- whether you have completed the welcome screen
- dark mode on or off
- your chosen app language

These settings stay on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

## Data Collection

TapTone does not require an account. TapTone does not collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, location data, contacts, health data or payment information.

## Third-Party Services

TapTone does not include advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking services. When you open the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use or Support links from the app, they open in your web browser on GitHub Pages, which is subject to [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children's Privacy

TapTone includes a Kids color test and is suitable for children. TapTone does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes To This Policy

## Disclaimer

TapTone is an assistive tool, not a medical device. It helps enhance color differences, but results may vary depending on lighting conditions, camera quality, screen calibration, and individual color vision. TapTone does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Do not rely on TapTone for medical, safety-critical, or emergency decisions.
