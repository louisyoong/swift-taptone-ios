## Frequently Asked Questions

<div class="faq" markdown="1">

### The Detect or Simulate tab says "Camera unavailable"

TapTone needs camera permission for these tabs. Open your device's **Settings › TapTone** and turn on **Camera**, then reopen the app.

</div>

<div class="faq" markdown="1">

### I can't choose a photo in the Assist tab

Tap **Upload Photo** and pick an image. If nothing appears, open **Settings › TapTone › Photos** and allow access.

</div>

<div class="faq" markdown="1">

### How do I get the most accurate color in Detect?

- Use even daylight or white light where possible. Warm or colored lighting changes how colors look to the camera.
- Move close so the color fills the small circle in the center of the target.
- Avoid shiny spots and shadows.
- Hold steady for a moment. The reading settles after about half a second.

Tap the copy button to copy the hex code of the current color.

</div>

<div class="faq" markdown="1">

### How do the Assist filters work?

Take or upload a photo, then pick a filter (Deuteranomaly, Protanopia or Tritanopia) to enhance color differences for that type of color vision. Press and hold the eye button on the photo to compare with the original.

</div>

<div class="faq" markdown="1">

### Which color test should I take?

- **Adult Test** — type the number hidden in each dot plate.
- **Kids Test** — tap the animal hidden in each plate.
- **Shapes Test** — tap the shape hidden in each plate.

For best results, turn screen brightness up, turn off Night Shift and True Tone, and hold the phone at a normal reading distance. The tests are for awareness only and are not a medical diagnosis.

</div>

<div class="faq" markdown="1">

### How do I change the language or turn on dark mode?

Open the **Settings** tab in TapTone. You can choose from 13 languages and switch dark mode on or off.

</div>

<div class="faq" markdown="1">

### Does TapTone collect my data?

No. Photos, camera images and test answers stay on your device. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy-policy/).

</div>

## Disclaimer

TapTone is an assistive tool, not a medical device. It helps enhance color differences, but results may vary depending on lighting conditions, camera quality, screen calibration, and individual color vision. TapTone does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Do not rely on TapTone for medical, safety-critical, or emergency decisions.
