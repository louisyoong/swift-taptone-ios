## Purpose

TapTone is an assistive accessibility tool that helps users distinguish color differences. It includes:

- **Assist** — photo filters that enhance color differences
- **Detect** — live camera color naming with hex and RGB values
- **Simulate** — live camera simulations of color vision deficiencies
- **Color Test** — Adult, Kids and Shapes color vision screening tests

## Color Vision Test Is Not A Diagnosis

The color vision tests are for fun and awareness only. They are inspired by dot-pattern plates but are not a validated clinical test, and results depend on your screen, brightness and lighting. A test result is not a diagnosis. See an eye care professional for an official color vision assessment.
{: .callout .warn}

## No Medical Advice

TapTone is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Results may vary depending on lighting conditions, camera quality, screen calibration, and individual color vision.

## Color Detection Accuracy

Detected color names and values are estimates. Phone cameras adjust exposure and white balance automatically, and colored lighting changes how things appear, so a reading may not match the true color of an object.

## Appropriate Use

Do not rely on TapTone for medical, safety-critical, emergency, or professional decisions where an inaccurate color reading could cause harm or loss — for example, reading traffic signals, wiring, medication, food safety indicators or warning labels. Always use appropriate professional equipment and judgment when accuracy matters.
