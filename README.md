# TapTone

TapTone helps colorblind people tell colors apart. It's a native iOS app built with SwiftUI.

## Features

- **Assist:** take or upload a photo and apply filters that enhance color differences for deuteranomaly, protanopia and tritanopia. Press and hold to compare with the original.
- **Detect:** point the camera at anything to see its color name, hex code and RGB values live, and copy the hex code with one tap.
- **Simulate:** see the world through the camera as people with different types of color vision deficiency see it.
- **Color Test:** dot-plate screening tests in three modes.
  - **Adult:** type the hidden number.
  - **Kids:** tap the hidden animal.
  - **Shapes:** tap the hidden shape.
- **13 languages**, plus light and dark mode.

## Privacy

TapTone works entirely on your device. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Photos, camera images and test answers never leave your phone.

## Requirements

iOS 26 or later. Open `TapTone.xcodeproj` in Xcode 26 or later to build. The Detect and Simulate tabs need a real device because the Simulator has no camera.

## Disclaimer

TapTone is an assistive tool, not a medical device. The color tests are for awareness only and are not a diagnosis. Results may vary with lighting, camera quality, screen calibration and individual color vision. Do not rely on TapTone for medical, safety-critical or emergency decisions.
